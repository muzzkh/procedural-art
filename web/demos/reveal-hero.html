How this page works

The hero is one canvas with ordinary HTML text over it. Two versions of the same generated design are drawn once onto hidden canvases. Each frame the quiet version is copied to the screen, and the loud version is copied on top through a blob-shaped hole that follows the pointer.

The animation stops when the hero is scrolled out of view, the canvas is redrawn when the window changes size, and visitors who have asked their system for reduced motion get a still image.
